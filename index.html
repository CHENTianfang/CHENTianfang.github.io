TIANFANG CHEN

I am an MPhil student in Linguistics, Philology and Phonetics at the University of Oxford, where I work with Daniel Altshuler. Before coming to Oxford, I studied Logic and Psychology at Sun Yat-sen University.

My research lies at the intersection of cognitive science, computational pragmatics, and philosophy of language. I am interested in how conventional meaning and pragmatic inference interact in communication.

I also serve as Treasurer of the Linguistics Association of Great Britain Postgraduate Student Committee.

Email: tianfang.chen [at] ling-phil.ox.ac.uk.
